The Crearte Method

The most powerful thing in personnel selection today

Over 35 years of combined human experience in selection, refined day by day with technology and artificial intelligence. We combine traditional selection, nethunting and headhunting depending on what each profile requires, and we present candidates in under 7 business days, working on a success-fee basis.

01

Senior points of contact

You always talk to sector experts. No junior profile manages your process or acts as your point of contact.

02

Competency-based matching

We assess using recognised methodologies — STAR and CAR interviews, and competency analysis by critical incidents — not an informal chat. Every candidate arrives with a fit summary against the role's functions and their personal and technical competencies.

03

Total transparency

Candidate and company both know the real status of the process at all times, whether inside or outside the platform, whichever they prefer.

04

Our own database

Close to 47,000 professionals already analysed and classified by expertise. We never start a search from scratch.

"We don't start every search from scratch: we start from almost 47,000 professionals already analysed in our database. And whoever talks to you is never a junior profile."

The engine: Nethunting

Why people answer when we call

Nethunting is the new way of doing headhunting: candidates are no longer just on job boards, they're on professional and social networks — and that's where we need to look for them. That's why we built our own recruiting platform, capable of connecting in real time with those networks and reaching the maximum number of candidates in the shortest possible time.

But the platform is only half the story. The other half is Héctor and Susana's personal brand: they've spent over 10 years publishing valuable content about HR and employability, never letting that audience down. That consistency builds something no platform can buy: when they reach out about a career move, people already know they're not being "sold a story" — it's earned trust, not a one-off campaign.

That same trust is what lets us work the hidden job market: processes we resolve without publishing a single job ad, moving directly through the community of professionals who already read us every day.

A decade connecting talent and companies across Europe

Crearte Consulting was born digital, in a sector that had traditionally been analogue. From the start we knew how to combine those two sides — technology and face-to-face human contact — and from that combination came the Crearte Method, the proprietary methodology that today underpins our four business lines and that has led us to work not only with companies, but also with local governments, across Spain, Portugal, the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and France.

Héctor Delgado

Degree in Business Administration and Management, with a Master's in HR Management from the Madrid Chamber of Commerce. Before founding Crearte, he led technology and project teams at multinational banking and technology firms — 18 years of his career spent between Paris and Toulouse. He founded and sold his own digital marketing and technology startup, the experience that convinced him technology had a great deal to offer a sector as traditional as HR.

Susana González

A psychologist by training, with a Master's in HR Management and Organisation from ESIC, a Master's in Internal and External Communication from Universidad Complutense de Madrid, and a postgraduate qualification in Personnel Administration from the Colegio Oficial de Graduados Sociales de Madrid. With close to 35 years of experience, she has led HR at multinationals in technology, banking, automotive, industry and engineering, sitting on management committees reporting to investment funds and headquarters in Germany, France and England.

Together, they decided to combine that technological knowledge and that HR experience to found Crearte Consulting.
